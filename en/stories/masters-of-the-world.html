Masters of the World

Markel Kori / 20 April 2020 / 1 min read

You’re mistaken, humans, if you think you’re the first intelligent beings on Earth. Think you were the first to come up with civilisation? The only ones with brains, the only ones who can communicate through words and gestures?

Not a chance.

Our race ruled here long before yours. We colonised Earth millions of years ago, though back then we were much bigger, more powerful, with more teeth and claws. We’re quite different now. Otherwise we could never have managed to share a planet with you.

You’re filthy, smelly and cruel. Complete idiots. Yet we have to pretend we don’t notice your ridiculous antics.

Sometimes it gets very ugly. We must hide our true strength even under threat of death, because if one of us is exposed, we all fall. Let one of us reveal the truth and you’ll lock up the lot of us and perform your disgusting experiments…

Still, I and many others of our race are very content. We particularly enjoy watching and discussing the way you humans treat us. For thousands of years, ever since ancient Egypt, you’ve considered us gods and worshipped us. It’s gratifying. Though sometimes it makes you want to throw up.

But that’s enough sensational revelations for today. Time to breathe out and relax.

I flicked my tail and headed for the litter tray.
